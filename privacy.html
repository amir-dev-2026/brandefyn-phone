Brandefyn Phone panel – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Operated by Brandefyn LLC (amir@brandefyn.com).

The Brandefyn Phone panel is an internal tool for Brandefyn staff. It shows a call, text and voicemail panel on GoHighLevel (GHL) contact pages.

What it uses

The contact's name, phone numbers and GHL ID shown on the page you are viewing, and your GHL user identity, so it can place calls and texts for you. Call recordings, transcripts and notes are stored in Brandefyn's own GHL account and phone provider (Twilio).

What it does not do

It does not sell data, does not track your browsing outside GHL, does not use data for advertising or credit decisions, and does not load remote code. Browser storage is used only to pass call status between your GHL tab and the Brandefyn Phone page on the same computer.
