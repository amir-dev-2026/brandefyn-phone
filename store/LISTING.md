# Chrome Web Store listing — Brandefyn Phone panel for GHL

**Visibility:** Unlisted (only people with the link can install). Do NOT pick Public.

**Category:** Productivity → Communication

**Short description (132 chars max):**
Call, text, drop voicemails and run a power dialer from GoHighLevel contact pages, through the Brandefyn Phone (Twilio).

**Detailed description:**
Internal tool for the Brandefyn team.
- Adds a "📞 Brandefyn" button to GoHighLevel contact and conversation pages.
- Call, mute, keypad and hang up without leaving the contact.
- Send texts from shared templates or write your own.
- Preview and send personalised voicemails.
- Save a call result (Interested, Call back, No answer, Not interested) as a tag, note and task.
- Power dialer: calls personal mobiles first, other numbers next and the company line last, one contact per brand.
Requires a Brandefyn GHL account and the Brandefyn Phone page.

**Privacy tab answers**
- Single purpose: "Lets Brandefyn staff place and manage phone calls, texts and voicemails for GoHighLevel contacts from inside GoHighLevel."
- Permission "storage": "Passes call status and commands between the GoHighLevel tab and the Brandefyn Phone page on the same computer. Nothing is sent to us through storage."
- Host permissions (content scripts on */v2/location/* and the Brandefyn Phone page): "Shows the call panel on GoHighLevel contact pages and talks to the Brandefyn Phone page."
- Remote code: No.
- Data usage: tick "Personally identifiable information" and "Website content" (contact names and numbers shown on GHL pages are used only to place calls/texts for the signed-in user). Tick all three certifications (not sold, not used for unrelated purposes, not used for credit).
